# 90-Day Editorial Calendar (Skeleton)

This is a *skeleton*, not a schedule. The editor swaps pieces around
based on what's landing in the comment section, what's in the news,
and what the research bot proposes. What matters is the *shape* over
30-day windows: enough variety to feel alive, enough repetition to
build a brand.

## Rotation rule

Across every 7-day window, publish at least:

- 2 education / children pieces
- 1 innovation / recent invention piece
- 1 creative-thinking or methods piece
- 1 AI / thinking-vs-machine piece
- 1 Bharat-specific piece (economy, policy, culture, ecosystem)
- 1 guest-voice or field-report (weeks 3+)

If a topic is trending, we let it eat two slots — but never three in a
week.

## Weeks 1–4 (Launch)

Focus: **prove Inovers Journal is different**. Publish only pieces
that would embarrass a hot-take blogger to have on their site.

- W1 Mon — Seed: Why Indian schools kill creativity (published day 0)
- W1 Tue — Seed: AI is not the enemy of innovation
- W1 Wed — Seed: The 12-year-old who ran a real R&D project
- W1 Thu — "The five best questions to ask a bored 8-year-old"
- W1 Fri — "How Atal Tinkering Labs actually work — three schools we visited"
- W1 Sat — "A brief history of Jugaad — what we get wrong about it"
- W1 Sun — off (or comment digest)
- W2 — one AI-in-schools piece, one profile of an Indian teacher-innovator,
  one methods post ("Constraint-driven thinking, for kids"), one policy
  read ("NEP 2020, three years in"), one book recommendation post.
- W3 — first guest voice (a working teacher), one profile of a young
  builder, one contrarian methods post.
- W4 — a public reader Q&A digest.

## Weeks 5–8 (Momentum)

Focus: **turn readers into commenters**. Every post ends with a real,
answerable question. Editor replies to every top-comment within 24h.

- Introduce a weekly "Field notes" column: 800 words from someone
  actually doing the thing (a teacher, a founder, a student).
- Add one "Reader story" a week from the comment section — with
  permission, of course.

## Weeks 9–12 (Depth)

Focus: **produce one landmark piece per month**. 3,000+ words, original
reporting, worth linking to for the next 3 years.

- Sample landmark titles for the first three months:
  - "Inside three Indian schools that quietly rebuilt their curriculum"
  - "The state of AI literacy in Class 9 (we tested 400 students)"
  - "Who is teaching our teachers to teach thinking?"

## Guardrails

- **Never** publish a piece without a specific human's byline on the
  final draft. "Inovers Editorial" is a byline of last resort, not a
  default.
- **Never** ship on Sundays before month 3 unless something newsworthy
  demands it. The rhythm builds trust; the exceptions build attention.
- **Always** send the newsletter Thursday 10:00 IST. Nothing else.
- **Never** run a post through more than one AI-heavy revision. Voice
  erodes fast.
