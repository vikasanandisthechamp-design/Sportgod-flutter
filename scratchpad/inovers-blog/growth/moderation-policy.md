# Inovers Comment Policy

Public link from every post footer. Also shown at signup.

---

Inovers is an ideas community. The comments are where readers push each
other's thinking forward. That only works if the space is safe, honest,
and civil. This page explains what we allow, what we don't, and how
moderation works.

## What we welcome

- Disagreement with the author. Even sharp disagreement.
- Additional evidence — a paper, a book, a real story we missed.
- Questions, especially the ones you're a little embarrassed to ask.
- First-person accounts from your school, your classroom, your child,
  your community.
- Corrections to factual errors. We fix them and thank you in the piece.

## What we don't allow

- **Personal attacks.** Attack ideas, not people. "This argument is
  weak because X" is welcome. "You are stupid because X" is not.
- **Discrimination.** Any comment that targets people by caste,
  religion, region, gender, sexuality, disability, or age is removed
  and the account is suspended.
- **Doxing.** Sharing someone's private information (address, phone,
  workplace contact) is an instant permanent ban.
- **Spam or self-promotion outside context.** Links to your business
  in a comment about someone else's essay = removed.
- **Automated or ChatGPT'd comments.** If the comment reads like it was
  auto-generated, we remove it. This is a community of people.
- **Deliberate misinformation.** Repeating a factual claim after being
  shown that it's wrong.

## How moderation works

- **First line of defense: you.** Every comment has a "Report" button.
  Three unrelated reports auto-hide the comment for editor review.
- **Second line: our editors.** They read every reported comment
  within 24 hours (usually much faster). Actions taken are: nothing,
  hide, remove, warn the author, suspend the account.
- **Third line: appeals.** If your comment was removed and you think
  the decision was wrong, email **editorial@inovers.in** with the
  comment URL. Editors reply within 3 working days.

## Bans

- **First violation:** a warning + comment removed.
- **Second violation:** 30-day suspension.
- **Third violation, or any doxing / discrimination:** permanent ban.

We do not owe anyone a platform. We do owe everyone a fair process.

## Your data

Comments you post are public and attributed to your account. If you
delete your account, comments are shown as "[deleted]" but the thread
shape is preserved so the conversation still reads. See
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the full picture.
